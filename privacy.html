Privacy Policy

How Realty Homes collects, uses and protects your personal information.

Contact Us Read Terms & Conditions
Applies to: all Realty Homes visitors and account holders Last updated: August 10, 2026

1Introduction

Realty Homes ("we", "us", "our") operates the Realty Homes website and related services (the "Service"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you visit or use the Service, how we use it, and the choices you have.

By accessing or using the Service, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the Service.

2Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, as well as information collected automatically when you use the Service.

Information you provide

  • Account details — your name, email address and password when you create an account.
  • Contact and enquiry information — details you submit through forms, such as viewing requests, valuation requests, consultations and newsletter subscriptions.
  • Saved homes and preferences — homes you save, searches you store and other settings you choose.
  • Communications — messages you send to us or to our agents.

Information collected automatically

  • Usage data — pages you visit, listings you view and how you interact with the Service.
  • Device and technical data — browser type, operating system, IP address and similar details.
  • Cookies and similar technologies — as described in Section 4 below.

3How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to operate and improve the Service, including to:

  • Provide, maintain and personalize the Service and your account.
  • Respond to your enquiries, requests and feedback.
  • Connect you with agents and facilitate viewings, valuations and consultations.
  • Send you newsletters, updates and marketing communications you have requested.
  • Analyze usage, detect fraud and abuse, and maintain security.
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms & Conditions.

4Cookies

We use cookies and similar technologies to keep you signed in, remember your saved homes and preferences, and understand how the Service is used.

Most browsers let you control cookies through their settings. You may be able to block or delete cookies, but some features of the Service may not work properly without them.

5Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information in the following circumstances:

  • With agents and service providers — when necessary to respond to your enquiry or provide a service you requested.
  • For legal reasons — when required by law, legal process or regulation, or to protect the rights, safety and property of Realty Homes, its users or the public.
  • Business transfers — in connection with a merger, acquisition, reorganization or sale of assets, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction.
  • With your consent — where you have given us permission to share specific information.

6Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information against unauthorized access, loss, alteration or disclosure. No method of transmission over the Internet or method of electronic storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7Your Rights and Choices

Depending on where you live, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including the right to:

  • Access, correct or update the information we hold about you.
  • Request deletion of your information, subject to legal obligations.
  • Opt out of marketing communications at any time.
  • Restrict or object to certain processing of your information.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details in Section 10 below. We may ask you to verify your identity before acting on your request.

8Third-Party Links

The Service may contain links to third-party websites and services, such as maps, social media and external listings. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those third parties. We encourage you to review their privacy policies before providing them with any information.

9Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can delete it.

10Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page and, where appropriate, notify you by email or a notice on the Service. Your continued use of the Service after changes take effect means you accept the updated policy.
